SyllabusAI

In Progress

AI-powered study planning tool that creates personalized learning schedules from course syllabi.

TypeScript AI/LLM Calendar Integration

SyllabusAI is an AI-powered study planning tool designed to solve a universal student problem: turning a dense course syllabus into a realistic, personalized study schedule.

The Problem

Every semester, students receive syllabi packed with readings, assignments, and exam dates. Most students either procrastinate or create overly ambitious schedules they abandon within a week. There is no tool that intelligently breaks down academic workloads into manageable daily tasks.

The Solution

Upload your syllabus. SyllabusAI reads it, understands the structure, and generates a personalized study plan that:

  • Distributes workload evenly across available study days
  • Accounts for difficulty — harder topics get more time
  • Integrates with your calendar — respects existing commitments
  • Adapts to feedback — reschedules when you fall behind

Origin

This project was born from my own experience as a student at the Open University of Israel, juggling Calculus 2 and Mechanics alongside building AI systems and trading algorithms. I needed this tool, so I am building it.
